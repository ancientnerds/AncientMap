## 1. Purpose and the four streams

The build splits Theo into research only. M3 runs on the VPS, stores a dossier, and ends in status
`researched`. A local Claude Code session then writes, checks and publishes the paper through gated VPS
CLIs. The paper page shows evidence anchors, corrections, videos and the AI disclosure. The local
studio turns a paper into a YouTube episode: case file, script, voice, captures, timeline, a Remotion
render on the NVIDIA RTX 3080, and an upload package.
