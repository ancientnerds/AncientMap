1. SACRED SITES
   Our claim: "Educational use (scraping with attribution)"
   ACTUAL: All Rights Reserved - explicit prohibition on scraping
   VERDICT: >>> WRONG - MAJOR VIOLATION <<<

   Details: Their Terms of Use (sacredsites.com/terms_of_use.html) explicitly
   prohibit "data mining, robots, or similar data gathering and extraction
   tools." The site cannot be "reproduced, duplicated, copied, sold, resold,
   visited, or otherwise exploited" without prior written consent. No part
   may be "reproduced or stored in any other website."

   ACTION REQUIRED: Remove this source or obtain written permission.
   Risk level: HIGH

2. MEGALITHIC PORTAL
   Our claim: "Free KMZ download" / "Various"
   ACTUAL: Restrictive copyright - personal use only
   VERDICT: >>> WRONG - POTENTIAL VIOLATION <<<

   Details: Terms state "no part of this site may be reproduced or stored
   in any other website or included in any public or private electronic
   retrieval system." Data downloads are for personal use only. Republishing
   in our database likely violates terms. The free KMZ download does not
   grant redistribution rights.

   ACTION REQUIRED: Contact megalithic.co.uk for redistribution permission,
   or remove this source.
   Risk level: HIGH

3. UNESCO WORLD HERITAGE
   Our claim: "Open with attribution" / "Public Domain"
   ACTUAL: Restricted - requires prior written authorization
   VERDICT: >>> WRONG <<<

   Details: UNESCO WHC explicitly states: "Any republication, online or in
   any other form, of any UNESCO/WHC data requires prior written
   authorization." Cannot sell, license, or assign use of materials.
   Syndication feeds are available for "personal, non-commercial use,
   without fee" but each use must include specific copyright notice and
   link back. Our configs.py incorrectly labels this "Public Domain."

   ACTION REQUIRED: Either obtain written permission from UNESCO, or
   ensure we only use syndication feeds with proper attribution notice
   "Copyright (c) 1992-2026 UNESCO/World Heritage Centre. All rights
   reserved." Update license field from "Public Domain" to
   "UNESCO Syndication Terms (non-commercial, with permission)".
   Risk level: MEDIUM-HIGH

4. ARACHNE (iDAI.objects)
   Our claim: "CC-BY" (ingester) / "CC BY-NC-SA 3.0" (configs.py)
   ACTUAL: CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 DE (per re3data registry)
   VERDICT: >>> WRONG (both versions!) <<<

   Details: The re3data registry (authoritative source for research data
   repositories) lists Arachne as CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 DE. This is the German
   law version with THREE restrictions:
     - NC: Non-commercial only
     - ND: No derivatives (we cannot transform/adapt the data)
     - DE: German law applies

   The ND restriction is the most problematic: if we're transforming Arachne
   records into our unified format, that may constitute a derivative work.
   The ingester header says "CC-BY" (far too permissive) and configs.py says
   "CC BY-NC-SA 3.0" (wrong type - SA not ND).

   ACTION REQUIRED: Update to "CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 DE". Assess whether our
   data transformation violates the NoDerivatives clause. Consider contacting
   DAI for clarification on whether database integration counts as a
   derivative work.
   Risk level: MEDIUM-HIGH

SHARE-ALIKE (SA) IMPLICATIONS
================================================================================

Sources with SA requirements:
  1. DARE (CC BY-SA 3.0)
  2. Vici.org (CC BY-SA 3.0)
  3. OSM (ODbL - similar to SA)
  4. Seshat Equinox (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)
  5. David Rumsey (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0)
  6. Mycenaean Atlas (CC BY-SA 4.0)
  7. ROCEEH ROAD (CC BY-SA 4.0, if confirmed)

SA means: If we create derivative works from these sources, the
derivatives must be shared under the same or compatible license.
For a mixed-source database, this can create "license pollution"
where SA from one source infects the entire combined work.

Mitigation: Keep SA-licensed data clearly separable from other data.
Display source attribution per-record, not just globally.
