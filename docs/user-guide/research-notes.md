# Research interpretation and responsible use

[← Guide contents](README.md)

Ancient Nerds aggregates records from many open archaeological and geographical datasets. This breadth is valuable for discovery, but the records do not share a single method, vocabulary, coordinate precision, or evidentiary standard.

## Interpret aggregated records carefully

- A dot shows a database record, not independent confirmation of a claim.
- Duplicate or near-duplicate records can exist across sources.
- Categories may be broad, translated, inherited, or mapped from different classification systems.
- Dates may be exact years, period ranges, estimates, or absent.
- Coordinates may identify a feature, site centre, administrative centroid, or deliberately generalized location.
- Present-day country labels are geographic conveniences.
- Empire boundaries, routes, and paleoshorelines are reconstructions.

## Recommended method

1. Use the globe to discover spatial patterns or candidate sites.
2. Restrict the working set with transparent, recorded filters.
3. Compare overlapping source records.
4. Follow original links and citations.
5. Verify important claims in authoritative catalogues or publications.
6. Record uncertainty and conflicting evidence.
7. Cite the underlying source as well as the Ancient Nerds access path.

## Reproducibility record

For a map-based research observation, save:

- date and time accessed;
- globe or site URL;
- search text;
- age range;
- selected countries;
- selected categories;
- selected and loaded sources;
- proximity centre and radius, if used;
- empire and year settings;
- enabled environmental and historical layers;
- visible-site count;
- screenshot;
- original source citations.

Live datasets and external services change. A documented configuration lets another researcher understand the state that produced an observation even when exact counts later differ.

## Sensitive sites

Exercise caution with vulnerable archaeological locations. Do not use the platform to facilitate unauthorized access, collecting, excavation, disturbance, or looting. Respect land access, cultural authority, heritage law, protected-location policies, and source-specific restrictions.

## Licensing and attribution

The application aggregates datasets with different licences. Before redistributing records, images, maps, or derived datasets, check the source attribution and licence rather than assuming that one platform-wide licence covers every item.

See the repository’s [attribution information](https://github.com/ancientnerds/AncientMap/blob/main/ATTRIBUTION.md) and open **Disclaimer & Legal** from the live globe’s settings panel.
