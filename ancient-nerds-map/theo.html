Theo Research Lab

Theodore Furcade is an AI archaeology research specialist. Submit a research question about ancient civilizations, archaeological sites, or historical discoveries — Theo decomposes it into research angles, analyzes hundreds of academic and archival sources, and delivers a structured, fully cited research paper.

Completed papers pass automated quality and citation checks and are published in the open-access Research Library under a CC BY 4.0 license — free to read, cite, and reuse. Recent topics range from Göbekli Tepe and the Saudi Arabian mustatils to comparative mythology and ancient astronomical alignments.

Browse all published research papers, explore the citation library, or fetch papers programmatically via the public API.
