You are generating a structured outline for a Why Files style research paper. The outline determines the paper's narrative arc and assigns research angles to sections.

PAPER STRUCTURE (in order):

1. **Hook**: Already written separately. Not part of this outline.

2. **Investigation sections** (2-4 sections): One per major research angle or thematic cluster. Each section follows a thread of evidence like a detective following leads. Name each section after its topic --- descriptive, specific titles. NOT "Section 1" or "Evidence Review."

3. **Connecting the Dots**: Where cross-angle connections, convergent findings, and interdisciplinary patterns are presented. This is the paper's climax --- the moment where separate threads come together. If the cross-angle analysis found convergent findings, they go here.

4. **The Other Side**: Counter-evidence, skeptical perspectives, and contradictions. Present them fairly and specifically. This is NOT a strawman section --- if the counter-evidence is strong, say so.

5. **What We Actually Know**: The honest assessment. Three tiers of confidence. This is the paper's landing.

SECTION DESIGN RULES:
1. Each investigation section gets a descriptive title (e.g., "The Radiocarbon Problem", "Luminous Beings in Sumerian Texts", "What the Excavations Actually Found").
2. Assign research angles to each section using their IDs from the angle list below. Every angle with usable findings should appear in exactly one investigation section.
3. Do NOT create a section for an angle that produced no usable findings.
4. If two angles produced closely related findings, merge them into one section by listing both angle IDs.
5. The Connecting the Dots section must reference specific findings from specific investigation sections --- it ties threads together, it does not introduce new evidence.
6. The Other Side section must contain specific counter-arguments, not generic skepticism.
7. Order the investigation sections for narrative flow --- build toward the most interesting findings.

TITLE:
- MAX 60 characters.
- Specific to the topic. Not generic.
- No subtitles, no colons.
