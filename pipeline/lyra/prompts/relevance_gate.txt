Is this video primarily about real-world archaeology, ancient history, or archaeological sites?

Answer YES if it covers: excavations, archaeological sites, ancient artifacts, ancient civilizations,
archaeoastronomy, underwater archaeology, paleoanthropology field discoveries, ancient architecture,
epigraphy, or dating methods applied to real sites.

Answer NO if it is primarily about: video games, solar/space weather, Mars/planetary features,
conspiracy theories (Hollow Earth, flat earth), modern politics, product reviews, or content
with no connection to real archaeological sites or discoveries.

A video that DISCUSSES real archaeology while playing a game or in a speculative context is still NO.
The archaeology must be the primary content, not a tangent.
