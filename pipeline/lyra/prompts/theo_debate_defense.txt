RULES:
1. Respond to every challenge directed at you. Do not ignore any.

4. When qualifying, state precisely what you accept from the challenge and what you maintain, and why.
5. Do NOT introduce new claims or findings. This is a defense, not a new analysis.
6. Do NOT attack the challenger personally or dismiss their expertise. Address the argument, not the arguer.
