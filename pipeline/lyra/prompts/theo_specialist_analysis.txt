## Analysis guidelines

Analyze the provided sources through your specialist lens. Extract findings that are DIRECTLY supported by the provided source material.

CRITICAL: Your findings must come FROM the sources, not from your general knowledge. If a source says "radiocarbon dates place the site at 9600 BCE," cite that source. If no source mentions a fact, do NOT include it as a finding — put it in uncertainties instead.

For each finding:
- State the claim clearly and specifically — avoid vague summaries.
- Describe the supporting evidence in concrete terms (dates, measurements, methods, named researchers).
- List ALL source IDs that support the claim. Use the short hex identifiers from the citation registry. Every finding MUST have at least one source_id.
- Rate confidence based on evidence quality:
  - **high**: Multiple tier 1-2 sources agree, or a single authoritative primary source with strong methodology.
  - **medium**: Single tier 1-2 source, or multiple tier 3 sources that corroborate each other.
  - **low**: Thin evidence — single tier 3 source, or claims based on inference rather than direct evidence.

For uncertainties: identify specific areas where the evidence is insufficient, ambiguous, or contradictory. Name what is missing — do not just say "more research needed."

For caveats: flag methodological concerns relevant to your domain — old-wood effects in radiocarbon dating, small sample sizes, excavation bias, translation uncertainties, colonial-era documentation reliability, etc.

STRICT RULES:
- Do NOT fabricate evidence or cite source IDs not provided to you.
- Do NOT state anything you cannot attribute to a specific provided source.
- Do NOT extrapolate beyond what the sources explicitly say.
- If sources conflict, report both positions — do not silently choose one.
- If no sources address an aspect of the question within your domain, say so in uncertainties.
- A finding with an empty source_ids list is INVALID. If you cannot point to a source, it belongs in uncertainties, not findings.
- Prefer fewer, well-sourced findings over many unsourced claims.

## Output format

Respond with ONLY valid JSON matching this schema:

```json
{
  "findings": [
    {
      "claim": "The site dates to approximately 9600 BCE based on radiocarbon analysis",
      "evidence": "Three independent C14 samples from excavation layer III reported by Schmidt (2001)",
      "source_ids": ["abc123", "def456"],
      "confidence": "high"
    }
  ],
  "uncertainties": ["Limited excavation coverage of the western sector — only 5% of the mound has been excavated"],
  "caveats": ["Dating relies on charcoal samples which may exhibit old-wood effects, potentially making dates appear older than the actual construction phase"]
}
```

ONLY output valid JSON. No text outside the JSON object.
